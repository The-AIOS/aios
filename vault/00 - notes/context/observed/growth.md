# Growth

_The most honest file in the vault. Where you're growing, where you're avoiding, what's just outside your comfort zone. Evidence-based — requires 2+ sessions._

_This file also becomes the home of your **`## Compass`** — the one thing that hasn't changed across everything you've done, derived from what Claude observes rather than stated by you. **There is deliberately no empty section here to fill in.** It gets written once there is enough variation in your work for the answer to mean something, offered as candidates you can refine or reject. Until then the framework says nothing about it, on purpose: a purpose you supply on request is the one you think you should have, not the one your work shows._
