## How this works

- **Declared context** lives in [[about_me|declared/]] — what you tell Claude
- **Observed context** lives here — what Claude learns through working together
- **Venture deep dives** live in `ventures/` — reference material for strategy and product work
- **Project-level context** lives inside each project — scoped knowledge for specific work

See [[vault-routine]] for the session cadence and command schedule.
