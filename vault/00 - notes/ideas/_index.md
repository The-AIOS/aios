# Ideas — Index

> Permanent notes promoted from daily notes. Each idea earns its own file once it's mature enough to stand alone.

---

## What Lives Here

Ideas are half-formed thoughts that appeared in daily notes and proved worth keeping. They get promoted here via the `/aios:graduate` command, which scans recent daily notes for recurring themes, interesting connections, or insights that deserve their own space.

This is the vault's long-term idea memory. Daily notes are ephemeral; ideas that land here persist.

---

## Convention

- **One idea per note** — keep it focused. If an idea has sub-ideas, link to them rather than nesting.
- **Clear title** — the filename should communicate the idea at a glance.
- **Tags** — tag with relevant domains so ideas surface during `/connect` and `/ideas` commands.
- **Links** — link back to the daily note where the idea first appeared, and forward to any project it feeds into.

---

## Current Contents

This folder populates as `/aios:graduate` runs. New ideas appear here over time.

---

## Lifecycle

1. An idea appears in a daily note (often in the Parking Lot or Radar section)
2. It recurs or deepens across multiple days
3. `/aios:graduate` identifies it and creates a permanent note here
4. The idea may eventually evolve into a project in `vault/00 - notes/projects/`
