# aios-strategy

> Research, advisory, deep dives. The "think harder before deciding" bundle.

Install this bundle if you do market research, give strategic advisory, or want structured deep-dives on companies and industries.

## Agents

| Agent | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[market-researcher]] | Deep 11-section McKinsey-style market intelligence — TAM, competitive landscape, market sizing, segmentation, regulatory environment, future outlook |
| [[consultant]] | Strategic advisory using established frameworks — Porter's five forces, jobs-to-be-done, growth strategy, Wardley mapping. Bring questions; get frameworks |
| [[company-analyst]] | Acquired.fm-style deep dives — history, strategy, moat (7 Powers), playbook grade, lessons. For competitive intelligence or due diligence |

## When to install

- You evaluate markets before entering them
- You advise on strategic decisions (own ventures or clients)
- You want to study a competitor with rigor, not vibes

## Dependencies

- **WebSearch + WebFetch** — for research
- **Obsidian MCP** — for routing findings to project notes
- **Optional: NotebookLM MCP** — for audio summaries of research outputs

See [[../_index|top-level agents registry]] for the full agent vocabulary.
